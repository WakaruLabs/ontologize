\section{Future work}
\label{sec:future}

\subsection{Identifiability first}
Before heads are named they must be reproducible, or it must be known
which are. The first layer partly is, and one head in it recurs in every
run; what lets that head recur when no other does, and whether training
pressure can extend it, are the first questions. The next training runs
use 128 entries per head and atoms without a decoder, the configuration
in which the GPT-2 hard codes were strongest, at two seeds each.

\subsection{Steering at scale}
The embedding-space steering instrument with SAE decoder rows and
supervised difference-of-means vectors alongside the Ontologizer's own
directions; per-entry effect consistency across contexts; and a lever on
collateral at the decision boundary, where the hard code's heads
currently sit. The single-layer hard variant, the one Ontologizer that
steers cleanly, is the reference to beat.

\subsection{Task reconstruction}
Recover what each reproducible head classifies: partition-against-label
mutual information led by topic and genre, which are what the embedding
is organized by; descriptions contrasting an entry with its siblings,
validated by blinded assignment accuracy against $1/k$; and parallel-text
invariance to separate content heads from form heads.

\subsection{Transcoder adaptation}
The architecture currently reconstructs an embedding. Adapting it to map
between layers, as transcoders do, is the obvious next target.

\subsection{A fresh evaluation set}
The softmax Ontologizers trained on the evaluation tail, so every
comparison against them is in-sample. Closing that needs a new source,
since the deterministic mC4 stream is exhausted by the training cache.
